\# 🏏 IPL Season-wise Team Performance Analysis (2008–2024)



\## 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed 1,090 IPL matches across 17 seasons (2008–2024) using Python

to uncover team performance trends, consistency patterns, and improvement 

trajectories across the top 6 IPL teams.



\## 🧰 Tools \& Technologies

\- \*\*Language:\*\* Python

\- \*\*Libraries:\*\* Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

\- \*\*Environment:\*\* Jupyter Notebook



\## 📂 Dataset

\- \*\*Source:\*\* Kaggle — IPL Complete Dataset

\- \*\*Matches:\*\* 1,090 clean matches across 17 seasons

\- \*\*Deliveries:\*\* 260,920 ball-by-ball records

\- \*\*Seasons Covered:\*\* 2008 to 2024

\## 🔍 Key Insights



\- \*\*KKR\*\* had the best single season in 2024 with \*\*78.6% win rate\*\*

&#x20; (11 wins in 14 matches) — highest ever by any team

\- \*\*SRH\*\* showed the most overall improvement across all seasons —

&#x20; from \*\*14.3% in 2008 to 56.2% in 2024\*\* (+41.9%)

\- \*\*RCB\*\* was the most inconsistent team across 17 seasons —

&#x20; ranging between 29% and 62% with no stable pattern

\- \*\*MI\*\* dominated from 2012–2020 but suffered their worst season

&#x20; in 2024 with only \*\*28.6% win rate\*\*

\- \*\*CSK\*\* was the most consistent team overall — never dropped

&#x20; below 40% (excluding the 2016 ban year)

\- \*\*2016 Ban Year\*\* — CSK and SRH were absent, clearly visible

&#x20; as a gap in the trend lines

\## 🎯 Conclusion

This analysis reveals that \*\*consistency beats peak performance\*\* in IPL.

CSK's steady approach outlasted MI's dominant era, while KKR's 2024 

resurgence proves that teams can reinvent themselves. RCB remains the 

most unpredictable team in IPL history.
